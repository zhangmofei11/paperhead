PAPERHEAD

About

This is an unofficial fan guide for PAPERHEAD, developed by te4m and published by HypeTrain Digital.

The game is a retro boomer shooter meets horror adventure FPS where you explore a surreal world made of cardboard and paper.

The free prologue "PAPERHEAD EP.0" is available on Steam.
