Tips & Tricks

Survival Tips

  • Move constantly - Standing still makes you an easy target
  • Use cover - Cardboard boxes provide excellent shelter
  • Listen to audio cues - Enemies often announce their presence
  • Watch the ground - Red stains indicate bleeding cardboard nearby

Combat Advice

  • Aim for weak points - Paper enemies have visible weak spots
  • Pistol is reliable - Always keep it ammo'd
  • Shotgun for crowds - Effective against groups
  • Rocket launcher - Save for bosses and tough enemies

Boss Fights

  • Learn attack patterns - Each boss has predictable moves
  • Dodge at the right time - Rolling has invincibility frames
  • Hit and run - Don't try to tank damage
  • Use the environment - Some bosses can be stunned

Exploration Tips

  • Check every corner - Secrets are often hidden
  • Break boxes - Many contain health or ammo
  • Read the notes - They provide hints and story
  • Return to areas - New items appear after events
