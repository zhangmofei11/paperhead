PAPERHEAD

Walkthrough

Getting Started

Your journey through the cardboard nightmare begins. Pay attention to your surroundings - nothing is as it seems.

  • Explore everything - Hidden items are often in unusual places
  • Watch for visual clues - Bleeding cardboard indicates secrets nearby
  • Save frequently - The game has multiple endings
  • Don't trust appearances - Paper enemies can transform

Combat Tips

  • Headshots matter - Deal more damage to paper enemies
  • Use the environment - Cardboard boxes can be used as cover
  • Ammo conservation - Some weapons have limited ammo
  • Dodge rolling - Essential for avoiding damage

Key Items

  • Cardboard Key - Opens red-marked doors
  • Tape Roll - Fixes broken pathways
  • Red Marker - Reveals hidden passages
  • Crumpled Note - Contains story clues

Endings Guide

Your choices affect which ending you get:

  • Accept Reality - Collect all memories, face the truth
  • Stay in the Dream - Refuse to acknowledge reality
  • Destroy the Paper World - Burn all evidence of the past
  • Hidden Ending - Find all secret letters
