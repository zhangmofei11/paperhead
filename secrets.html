Walkthrough Secrets Tips

Secrets

Secret Letters

Find all 5 hidden letters to unlock the secret ending:

  • Letter #1 - Behind the starting room bookshelf
  • Letter #2 - Inside a cardboard box in the hallway
  • Letter #3 - Under the bed in the bedroom level
  • Letter #4 - Behind the painting in the gallery
  • Letter #5 - In the paper shredder at the end

Hidden Rooms

  • The Author's Room - Behind the bookshelf in Level 2
  • Memory Vault - Throw items at the red-marked wall
  • Secret Armory - Jump on boxes to reach high platform
  • The Burning Room - Only accessible with all collectibles

Easter Eggs

  • Developer Room - Press all buttons in order in the control room
  • Hidden Message - Look at the wall in Level 3 for backwards text
  • Secret Song - Find the tape recorder and press play
  • cardboard Chicken - There's a reference to a famous game

Secret Endings

  • True Ending - Collect all 5 letters + complete all challenges
  • Bad Ending - Destroy the ending machine without collecting
  • Joke Ending - Found in the developer room
